# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: the owner or office manager of a residential HVAC and plumbing shop** with roughly 3–15 field technicians. They run the day from the owner dashboard: watching the dispatch board, approving held actions, checking money and agent traces, and asking the ops copilot. They are busy, interrupted and often on the phone. Their needs win design trade-offs.

Secondary audiences:
- **Homeowners** with a broken furnace, AC, water heater or drain. They reach the shop by browser voice call, web form or email, and later open signed links on a phone: booking confirmation, photo upload, good/better/best quote, live technician tracking, payment result. Often stressed, sometimes in an emergency (gas, CO, no heat in freezing weather).
- **Field technicians**, who use the Telegram bot (`/today`, `/onmyway`, `/arrived`, `/late`, `/done` + voice note, live location), not the web UI.
- **Evaluator** (Kidus, brain3.ai): reviews the demo video and source for the take-home. Not a design target in their own right; served by the product being genuinely good for the owner.

## Product Purpose

An AI front office that covers the dispatcher/CSR seat from the first call to the paid invoice: answer every contact 24/7 with safety advice first, triage with rules plus Gemini plus weather, book and re-plan the whole technician fleet, quote from photos using the price book, and turn a technician's voice note into a report, an invoice, a Stripe payment link and reminders. Success means no missed contacts, emergencies reached faster, fuller technician days, and faster cash, with the owner kept in control.

## Positioning

The owner stays in control through an explicit policy engine (auto / approve / never per action, with low model confidence downgraded to approval), and every agent step is traced and inspectable. Its claims are measured against itself: a day simulator compares the agent with a human-office baseline on the same demand and fleet, and triage is scored on labelled evals with a held-out split.

## Operating Context

- Owner dashboard routes: dispatch board (timeline per technician, map, unassigned, live activity), inbox & calls, approvals, quotes & invoices, agent traces, ROI & simulator, ops copilot, settings (integrations, proactive jobs, demo weather, policies, demo reset).
- Live updates over server-sent events; the dashboard is often left open all day.
- Customer links are signed tokens: `/c/track`, `/c/quote`, `/c/photos`, `/c/paid`; booking and voice at `/book`.
- Proactive jobs: minute watchdog (inbox, SLA, queue, payments), 06:00 weather scan, 06:30 morning plan; Cloud Scheduler in production.
- Company time zone America/Denver; the demo tenant is "Northline Heating & Plumbing", Denver.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: TanStack Start (React 19, Vite, Nitro), Tailwind 4, Postgres + Drizzle, Gemini (text, vision, Live voice), Telegram, OpenRouteService, Open-Meteo, Nominatim, Stripe test mode, optional Gmail/Calendar.
- UI components are to be migrated to **shadcn/ui** for consistency, as a final pass (user decision).
- Customer pages must work well on phones; owner dashboard is desktop-first but must remain usable on mobile.
- Voice runs in the browser only (no phone numbers / Twilio).
- Payments are Stripe test mode only.
- Product name: deliberately unnamed; refer to it as the "AI front office". Northline is the demo tenant, not the product.

## Evidence on Hand

- Day simulator results (agent vs human-office baseline) and triage eval results stored at runtime and shown on the ROI page.
- Real agent traces, messages, invoices and Stripe test sessions generated by using the app.
- Demo data is fictional (seeded customers at @example.com, fictional addresses). There are no real customers, testimonials, press, pricing or deployment claims; future work must not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. **Safety before efficiency.** Hazard advice comes first in every channel; safety rules are code, not model output.
2. **Owner in control.** Every side effect passes the policy engine; held actions are easy to see and decide.
3. **Show the work.** Decisions carry their reasons (why P1, why moved, why held) and full traces.
4. **Honest numbers.** ROI and accuracy come from real event history, the simulator or evals, with assumptions stated.
5. **Never block the day.** Missing integrations degrade to rules, estimates or outbox, never to a broken screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Customer-facing pages are used on phones by stressed people, sometimes in emergencies: plain language, large tap targets, and safety text that cannot be missed. No formal WCAG level has been committed to.
